Why Astronaut Health Monitoring Must Be Radiation-Resilient & Autonomous

NASA HRP 5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight

  • Space Radiation: Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) & Solar Proton Events (SPE) increase biological dose AND trigger Single-Event Upsets (SEUs) in avionics SRAM.
  • Distance from Earth: Mars transit reaches 22-minute one-way latency — flight surgeons cannot intervene live.
  • Altered Gravity Fields: Causes cephalad fluid shift (SANS), ~1–1.5%/mo bone mineral loss, and orthostatic intolerance.
  • Isolation & Closed Cabin: 16 orbital sunrises/day impair sleep; rising CO2 causes headache before SpO2 drops.

Why Heavy Learned Weights Fail Deep Space

SRAM Bit-Flip Vulnerability

Conventional neural networks store megabytes to gigabytes of learned parameters in mutable SRAM. A single unmitigated cosmic-ray bit-flip can silently distort triage thresholds when Earth links are unavailable.

AstroSoma Defense: 0 Bytes Weight RAM

Deterministic WERR-24 procedural escape boundary + transparent NASA-STD-3001 guardrails + CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE packet authentication.
